# What is Zotero?

Zotero is a tool designed for collecting, organizing, annotating, citing and sharing research papers. My classmates and I, being in the humanities department studying Intercultural Studies, European studies, etc.  have constant interactions with papers that require the appropriate documentation as this helps us to identify our references and have a more easy way to follow up with theoretical concepts. 


By 14th of December we need to present an assignment where  one of the points required is to provide a list of theoretical concepts with their reference. Next, we need to code our notes that could help our research. Zotero for this becomes a program where we can collect, organize, make notes and cite the papers that we engage on our research topic, this makes our research more efficient and it helps us to organize and keep track of our whole process.
